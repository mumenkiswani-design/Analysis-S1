Final Allocation Methodology

Both years use the same 2026 contribution structure for a like-for-like comparison.

1) For every account listed in Subagent Contribution 2026, account sales are multiplied by each Medical Rep's stated contribution percentage.
2) The same contribution percentages are applied to the same accounts in both S1 2025 and S1 2026.
3) Allocated sales are summed by Medical Rep, SKU, Brand, Area and period.
4) Accounts outside the contribution file follow the 2026 account / territory ownership mapping.
5) Hospitals are compared from Area = Hospitals. Subagent is compared from accounts allocated via Contribution % 2026.
